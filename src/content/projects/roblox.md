I received a text message with an AI generated image and the ask, "do you know how to make this real?" The image contained 10 crystal eggs, each emanating a glow of a unique element central to the experience of the Roblox egg hunt (earth, water, fire, air, love...). I helped assemble a small team of uniquely skilled artists/engineeers, and together, we made it real.

The result is a 20lb, leather bound display enclosure fit for a museum. Inside the enclosure, custom electronics provide a light show from 80 individually addressable RGBW LEDs, mounted and hidden to provide diffuse, reflected light, and controlled with a reed switch, ensuring the light show greets each open and consumes effectively no power when closed. Then the main attraction, 10 unique eggs containing custom artwork with over a billion voxels printed. Only a handful of people can print something like this, suspending full spectrum volumetric imagery, like a colorful CT scan, in a physical object to be held.
