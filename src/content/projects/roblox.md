I received a text message with an AI generated image and the ask, "do you know how to make this real?" The image contained 10 crystal eggs, each emanating a glow of a unique element central to the experience of the Roblox egg hunt (earth, water, fire, air, love...). I helped assemble a small team of uniquely skilled artists/engineeers, and together, we made it real.

![10 Roblox eggs illuminated on a cutting mat](/images/roblox-01.jpeg)

The result is a 20lb, leather bound display enclosure fit for a museum. Inside the enclosure, custom electronics provide a light show from 80 individually addressable RGBW LEDs, mounted and hidden to provide diffuse, reflected light, and controlled with a reed switch, ensuring the light show greets each open and consumes effectively no power when closed. Then the main attraction, 10 unique eggs containing custom artwork with over a billion voxels printed. Only a handful of people can print something like this, suspending full spectrum volumetric imagery, like a colorful CT scan, in a physical object to be held.

![laser cut enclosure in progress](/images/roblox-02a.jpeg)

![custom electronics and lighting for enclosure](/images/roblox-02b.jpeg)

The enclosure was designed to be a modular assembly, allowing for experimentation with the location and angle with which the LEDs illuminate the eggs. Each egg's custom artwork had unique properties for translucency and in final testing, the enclosure which was designed to fade into the background was outfitted with highly diffuse white cardstock to deliver a James Turrell effect when vacant.

![cross section of enclosure showing lighting angle, eggs, and battery placement](/images/roblox-03a.jpeg)

![instructions showing how the battery would be mounted to the enclosure](/images/roblox-03b.jpeg)

For robustness, the battery pack would be mounted to the enclosure itself, while the inner tray containing the electronics and lighting would slide in. The battery allows for the enclosure to be on display without any visible power source, while pass-through USB-C charging allows for continuous operation.

![CNC cutter with interior flocking material and laptop](/images/roblox-04a.jpeg)

![3D printer with egg trays](/images/roblox-04b.jpeg)

![electronics being soldered](/images/roblox-04c.jpeg)

This project applied a broad range of my skillsets from custom electronics and power management to, CAD, laser cutting, 3D printing, and lighting techniques. Moreover, I got to flex project management and team building for a one-of-a-kind display.
