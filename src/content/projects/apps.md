Small apps for everyday things.

Each one starts as something I wanted for myself, and each does one job. They all live together at [app.jonbobrow.com](https://app.jonbobrow.com/), signed "App by Jon Bobrow".

- [Now Departing](https://app.jonbobrow.com/now-departing/): live NYC Subway departure times for the train you're trying to catch, on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
- [Cookbo](https://app.jonbobrow.com/cookbo/): share a recipe link and it keeps the recipe and leaves the rest. No ads, no accounts, and your recipes stay yours as plain files.
- [PointerPals](https://app.jonbobrow.com/pointerpals/): your friends' cursors show up on your screen while they work, and fade away when they go still.
- [Evening Reader](https://app.jonbobrow.com/evening-reader/): save articles, books and PDFs, then read them offline on a warm amber page. Built for winding down.
- [Audio Bubble](https://app.jonbobrow.com/audio-bubble/): hear the people you're with, even in loud places. Coming soon.
- [NYC Parking](https://app.jonbobrow.com/nyc-parking/): alternate-side parking rules for every block in New York City on a live map, with a reminder before it's time to move the car.

Made with heart and AI. More on the way.
